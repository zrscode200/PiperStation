# Product

Piper Station is a central design and development workspace for one developer
working with Codex across registered projects. It connects exploration,
deliberate planning, bounded implementation, review, and durable resumption.

This branch targets Codex so its prompts and wiring can evolve together. The
products developed through Piper may use any language, platform, or runtime.
Project source stays in project repositories; the hub owns project context and
work records. Ordinary work stays lightweight and scales its records to need.

The generated hub's detailed product contract lives in `core/shared/PRODUCT.md`.
